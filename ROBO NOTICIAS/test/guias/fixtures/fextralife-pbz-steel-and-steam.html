Steel and Steam | Phantom Blade Zero

This page was last edited on 29 October 2026, at 02:01.
From Phantom Blade Zero

Steel and Steam in Phantom Blade Zero is a complete walkthrough for the chapter following Against the Flow, guiding players through the Sha-Chi Tower route, major side objectives, and key boss encounters. This guide covers Bell checkpoints, important NPC interactions, Tales progression, collectible Books, upgrade materials, and critical rewards such as Dread Axe, Twin Flesh Lilies, and Hunter’s Chain Sickle from The Hunt. 


Steel and Steam Walkthrough in Phantom Blade Zero

After completing the Chapter Against the Flow, exit the destroyed village. There will be a stone bridge that will lead you to the next area. But just before you cross that bridge, drop down from the bridge to pick up a few items. In the right corner is a chest that contains a Broadplate Metal. On the western corner of the bridge will be a Blood-Stopper Vine. The Blood-Stopper Vine is one of the key components for the Tale: Apprentice Healer. Lastly, climb back up and destroy the chained gate to find the Book: Fragments of the Mandate. There will also be a chest nearby that players can open to acquire a Focus Pill. 


Once that's done, cross the stone bridge and defeat the enemies using the Dread Axe. Destroy the chained gate using the Dread Axe, and you'll end up outside of a the Sha-Chi tower.


Upon entering this area, you'll find a Dying Mandate Disciple by a wooden cage. Speak to him, and he'll tell you to go around the Sha-Chi tower. Afterwards, continue pressing forward, and you'll reach the first Bell (Creek Valley Plains Entrance) in this area.


Near the Bell is a Panicked Mandate Disciple. Speak with him to learn about the monsters inside that tower. Once the conversation ends, the quest Deep in the Valley unlocks.


Continue moving straight, and a brief cutscene will take place with The Hunt crossing a bridge, killing everyone in its path. 


Enter the Steel Tower and find a way to the Hunt's Hideout

Upon entering this area, players will be facing off against multiple enemies. There are the Transcended monsters as well as the archers mentioned by the Brooding Old Archer. Players must defeat all of them to progress the quest.


Defeating all of the enemies can grant players a Blackstone Fragment. This will also progress the Tale: Purge the Sect. Additionally, climb up the staircase in the northwest section of the area to find the Bell (Ruthless Archer Camp). Right next to the Bell will be a treasure chest where you can acquire a Token of Pursuit.


While players can continue the Storyline, it is recommended to head back to the outpost and complete the Tale: Purge the Sect. Handing over the Bow to the Brooding Old Archer will grant players the Phantom Edge Feather Bow.


Head back to the Ruthless Archer Camp Bell. From the bell, drop down, follow the large metal tube. Climb up using the slope to reach a house where you can pick up the book Reflections.


Afterwards, cross the same bridge The Hunt went through. At the end of the bridge, pick up the Remnant Dusts and follow the single thread. This will lead you to the Bell: Valley Sha-Chi Tower 1st Floor.


On the first floor of the Sha-Chi Tower, players can pick up the Book: Mystery of Transcension. Players can also pick up the Book: Hua the Bride, just nearby the Mystery of Transcension.


Climb up the staircase and defeat the enemy waiting. Afterwards, climb the wall to reach the next floor. Circle around the floor to find the staircase. This will then lead you to the top of the tower. Additionally, just before climbing the staircase, players can find the Book: Mystery of Transcension nearby.


Once you're at the top, players will be able to find the next Bell. 


Next to the Bell is a Watchful Shadow Warden. Unlike the other wardens, the man at the top is not an enemy and has even managed to break the Mindseal. Exhaust the conversation with the warden to learn more about the Primal Stone. After speaking with him, the Tale: A Severed Mindseal unlocks.


Head back down using the stairs and then drop down once more. On the right corner of the platform, there will be a hole where you can jump into.


Head to the bottom to find a bridge. Sliding through that bridge will get you to a new area. At the bottom, players will encounter numerous enemies. Defeat all of them to progress. 


After defeating the last enemy, players will receive a section of the Book: Hua the Bride. While it may seem that the new area is large. every corner leads to. NOTE: Another section of the Book: Hua the Bride can be found just before entering Hua's lair.


Once that's done, players must enter Hua's Lair and defeat her. To learn more about Hua, Deadly Betrothed, kindly head over to our Boss Page. 


After defeating Hua, players will acquire the weapon Scarlet Wraithblade. Additionally, players can now interact with the Bell inside the Lair.


Head to the front of the lair and players will be able to find a story item, the Twin Flesh Lilies. 


Explore the corners of the arena to find all of the remaining Hua the Bride books.


Head back to the Outpost Bell and speak with Yu'En and give her the Flesh Lily. Exhaust the conversation with her, and the Tale: Flesh Lily progresses. While you're at the outpost, speak to the Swordsman Captain sitting near a large boulder to acquire the Book: Wulin Tales. This will also progress the Tale: Festering Secret.


Return to the top of Sha-Chi Tower. Next to the friendly warden, climb the wall to reach the higher platform and make your way to the connecting tube. Once you're at the ledge, move to the bottom using the ladder. 


Players will then have to fight an enemy at the bottom to obtain the Story Item - Sha-Chi Furnace Inspection Crank. By the edge, players can find a ladder that will lead them back to the Sha-Chi Tower's first floor.


Once you're back at the tower, insert the furnace crank into the middle mechanism. Doing so will grant you the item Primal Stone. 

Climb back to the top and give the Primal Stone to the Shadow Warden. Exhaust the conversation with him to progress the Tale: A Severed Mindseal. 


Head back to the bottom floor of the tower and use the ladder once more to climb up. Players will be able to see a worn-down man in the right corner. Speak with him to unlock the quest: Pillars of Kunlun. Pick up the item next to him to acquire the Book: Pillars of Kunlun.


Descend to the bottom of the path. There will be the Bell: Waterfall Bridge. Here, you'll be able to find a ladder and a bridge across. 

Drop down the ladder first and then turn left. There will be a staircase that will lead you to a chest. Defeat all of the enemies in the area and open the chest to acquire upgrade materials.

Climb back up and then cross the bridge. This will trigger the next objective for the chapter Steel and Steam.


Settle the Score with the Hunt!

Past the bridge, continue travelling straight. The path here is linear, and there are no enemies. Eventually, you will reach the next Bell: Great Waterfall.


After activating the Bell, make your way towards the Great Waterfall. Here, you will be greeted by The Hunt. Players will then have to fight against the Boss: The Hunt.

The Hunt can be a hard opponent. To learn more about it and how to defeat it, kindly head over to their Boss Page. After defeating its first and second phases, players will then acquire the item Hunter's Chain Sickle.

After picking up the Chain Sickle, players can do a bit of backtracking to acquire a few items. Head back to the very first starting area and move in the opposite direction to acquire the Pendant of Rightousness. From the Ruthless Archer Camp, use the Sickle to climb atop a roof to acquire a Mechanical Gear. From the Valley Sha-Chi Tower Summit, climb up the wall and use the Sickle near the connecting tube to acquire the Sha-Chi Tower Base Camp Gate Key.

After defeating The Hunt, head to the corner of the area and climb up the Great Waterfall. 

Inside, keep moving straight to reach a shrine where you can pick up the Book: Reflections. After picking up the book, move in the opposite direction and drop down the cliff.

At the bottom, players will be able to pick up 2 Exotic Copper Ingot. Next, turn right and follow the water until you find a waterfall. Below, players can pick up the Book: Fragments of the Mandate.

Past the Books, the path here will be pretty linear. There will be no enemies, so feel free to explore and pick up a few more items such as x2 Broadplate Metal, x2 Phantom Reforge Talisman, and a Traversal Talisman along the way. Once that's done, make your way towards the end of the cave. This will then complete the Chapter Steel and Steam.

Deep in the Valley

Deep in the Valley is another quest that can be completed once players have obtained the Hunter Chain's Sickle and the Sha-Chi Tower Base Camp Gate Key. We recommend clearing the area before progressing into the next chapter. To start, head to the Creek Valley Plains Entrance Bell and unlock the gate.


Upon entering the plains, turn right after your first encounter with the Transcended. This will lead you to a small waterfall area with enemies. By the waterfall, players can pick up a large sum of Remnant Dust.


Return back and press forward. After a while, players will find three pathways. Enter the eastern path and defeat all of the enemies. 


On the house on the right, players can pick up blabla, while the house on the left will contain the Crane Bell for that area.


Make your way to the house on the right (Players can also find a Traversal Talisman below this house) and use the connecting tube to reach the Blue-Robed Swordsman.. Speak with him to trigger the next objective of the Tale: Four Fierce Axes. 


Afterwards, make your way to the house with the Crane Bell. Interact with the Crane Bell (Long Pillar Overlook) to learn more about Soul's past. Once that's done, pick up the items behind the Bell to acquire the Book: Reflections and the Rusted Loong Key.


Head outside and use the bridge to traverse to the next area. Once you're past the bridge, every pathway here will lead to an already explored area. Just make sure to pick up a few important items. 


After traversing the wall, pick up the Congeleaf to progress the Tale: Apprentice Healer.


Near the Congeleaf is a locked gate. Players must use the Rusted Loong Key to enter. Navigate the long cave until you reach an enemy by the steel crates. Defeat the Transcended enemy to acquire a Sha-Chi Amber Fragment.


At the end of the cave will be the Bell: Weeping Creek Long Pillar. Additionally, just behind the Bell, will be the Book: Wulin Tales.


Here, players can also challenge The Gold Masked One by interacting with the Dragon Statue in the middle. Defeating the Gold Masked One will progress the quest: Bloodline Awakening and grant you the Counterstrike Bracers


Heading straight where the Dragon is facing will bring you back to the bridge. Return to where you found the Congeleaf before and drop down. Players will then have to defeat all of the enemies


Move to the water and just when you're near the waterfalls, more enemies will appear along with an elite Transcended. Defeat the enemies and open the chest near the scaffolding to acquire a Blackstone Fragment.


Once that's done, players can now head back to the plain's Crane Bell and head to the other side. 


Head to the furnace section and pick up the Book: Fragments of the Mandate on the ground.


Further head, there will be a large blocked section on the ground. To open this section, players must lure an exploding enemy into its vicinity and let it explode. This will unlock an elevator which will grant you access to the lower floor. Players can acquire the Ancient Armor Fragment at the bottom near a tree as well as find the Bell (Crimson Manor) for the area. Additionally, interacting with the door of the manor will unlock the quest Crimon Manor.


Head back up and start climbing the tower. There will be a staircase near the edge of the area.  Past the staircase, you will be greeted with more enemies. Defeating all of the enemies will grant the player a Blackstone Fragment. Once that's done, return to the table by the staircase and pick up the Book: The Four Axes Case Files on the table. 


Follow the path behind the staircase. This will lead you to the Bell: Valley Sha-Chi Tower Base.


Just on the left side of the bell, players will be able to find the Book: Mystery of Transcension. Additionally, across the bell, will be another crank we can use. Use the Sha-Chi Furnace Inspection Crank we got from before to obtain the Reflex Device.


Circle around the furnace and keep on scaling upwards. Players will be able to obtain multiple Upgrade Materials along the way. At the very top, there will be a chest that contains the Vessel Restorative.

Trophy and Achievement Guide | Phantom Blade Zero

This page was last edited on 31 October 2026, at 01:17.
From Phantom Blade Zero

Trophy and Achievement Guide in Phantom Blade Zero is a collection of all the trophies and achievements that can be acquired in the game. Phantom Blade Zero will be available on both PlayStation and PC (Steam and Epic Store Games), and this Trophy and Achievement Guide will list both types and state any differences, where applicable. This page will list all of the trophies and/or achievements, how to obtain all of them, and reach 100% completion. Phantom Blade Zero features a total of 66 Trophies or Achievements.

  • Platinum Trophies: 1
  • Gold Trophies: 3
  • Silver Trophies: 22
  • Bronze Trophies: 40

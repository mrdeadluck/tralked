Quick answer: Phantom Blade Zero has 66 Steam achievements, most of them from story progress, boss kills, side quests, upgrades and specific weapons. “Defy Destiny” unlocks once you have all the others.

Each achievement also has a trophy grade in the game. There are 40 Bronze, 22 Silver, 3 Gold, and 1 Platinum. Steam hides 31 of them until you unlock them, and all 31 are story beats and boss kills. The list breaks down into 14 story achievements, 18 boss achievements, 9 story and side quests, 8 upgrade and collectible achievements, 2 combat techniques, 14 weapon and Phantom Edge achievements, and Defy Destiny. The player percentages below are Steam’s global stats from launch day, 29 October 2026, so expect them to shift a lot over the first few weeks.

The story and boss tables below name places and bosses from later in the game.

Difficulty does not affect any Phantom Blade Zero achievement

No achievement description mentions a difficulty. That means you can earn every one of them on Wayfarer, Pathbreaker, Hellwalker, or 66 Days. See the difficulty modes guide to pick a setting.

Steam’s global unlock rate for each achievement is in the Steam % column of the tables below. Defy Destiny is the Platinum-grade achievement for unlocking the other 65.

Story achievements (14)

These unlock as the main story moves forward. Steam hides all of them except The Heart’s Journey. Two of them are Gold: With Heart and Blood and All Dreams Must End.

Boss achievements (18)

Steam hides every boss achievement until you earn it. Each one names the boss you need to defeat.

Boss achievements that need more than one kill

  • Master of the Misty Waters: Blackwater Leader You Loong, Zither Maiden Yan Qiansui, and the One-Eyed Archer, all in the Meigu Lake area.
  • The Four Freed: Their names are Yuzan, Yubi, Yuding, and Yucong.
  • The Two Blade Masters: Zhu Yuan and Commander Bi Feng.
  • Three Wardens Fall: The fallen wardens are Cavalrybane, Soulclaimer, and Heartpiercer.
  • Turning the Tide: You Loong, Blackwater Crew Leader, defeated two times.

Story and side quest achievements (9)

These are visible on Steam from the start. Each one unlocks when you finish the named quest. Secrets at the Heart of the Lake and Bloodline Awakening are listed under Story in the Quests tab, not with the tales. The Healer’s Legacy is the only one that needs three quests.

Here are the quest log objectives for each quest. Following them through to the end completes the quest.

Secrets at the Heart of the Lake

  • Find Firefly’s Memory Flowers, then enter Dusk’s Refuge.
  • Find a way into the Heart of the Lake and search there for the ritual site.
  • Gather all four Acolytes’ masks, then make your way into the Forbidden Ruins.
  • At Crimson Manor, locate the Crystal Eyeball [Yang], then fit it into Puppet No. 99.
  • Explore the canyon to find Dusk.

Bloodline Awakening

Find all five Lóng Pillars and complete their trials. After that, the quest sends you back to the Lóng Pillar twice, first after new discoveries and then to investigate.

Secrets of the Baishan Sect

  • Search the Mandate Encampment for the Baishan Sect Leader.
  • Examine Bi Taotao’s body, then defeat Bi Taotao’s puppet form.
  • Find Junior Sister Meng at the mountain lodge.

The Journey Home

  • Clear the Smuggler’s Tunnel and return to Old Man Gen.
  • Go back to Village of Lu and tell him about the passage.
  • Give the Wooden Comb to Old Man Gen at the Village of Lu Gate.
  • Find his old house in Pangzhen’s backstreets to learn where his wife is.
  • Find Old Man Gen in Pangzhen.

Young Master Lan

  • After clearing the Smuggler’s Tunnel, return to Lan Shaodi and tell him the path is clear.
  • Meet Lan Shaodi at his manor in Pangzhen.
  • Touch any bell, then return to the Lan Manor to investigate.
  • Go to the Lan Manor and talk things over with Lan Shaodi.

A Severed Mindseal

The friendly Shadow Warden needs a Primal Stone from each of three regions: Weeping Creek Valley, Pangzhen, and the Crimson Forest. Find a stone in each region and give it to the friendly Shadow Warden in that same region.

Flesh Lily, Apprentice Healer, and A Healer’s Mercy

  • Flesh Lily: Find the Flesh Lily for Yu’er and check on her father. After reaching Pangzhen, return to escort her home, then hand her the Flesh Lily.
  • Apprentice Healer: Find the three herbs from Yu’er’s medicinal recipe in Weeping Creek Valley and bring them to her.
  • A Healer’s Mercy: Go to the spot marked in Record of the Lost Thieves and treat their wounds, then tell Yu’er.

Quick Hand’s Request

For Lost and Found, collect every treasure in five regions: Crimson Forest, Village of Lu, Pangzhen, Meigu Lake, and Mirage Estate. Then deliver each region’s set to the merchant Quick Hand Zhang. All ten objectives, five collections and five deliveries, have to be done. Where each treasure sits is in the Quick Hand Zhang treasure locations guide.

Heart of the Banyan

This quest has one objective. Find the Human-Faced Banyan Trees.

Upgrade, collectible, and combat achievements (10)

Every achievement here is visible. Tempering improves weapons and Phantom Edges. Sha-Chi Ambers, Memory Flowers, and Blackstones are world collectibles. Accessories count as equipment; there are 66 total, but Collector’s Notes requires only 30.

Weapon and Phantom Edge achievements (14)

Each of these asks you to use a specific main Weapon or Phantom Edge, which is the secondary Sha-Chi weapon, and pull off one of its moves. The weapons list covers what every one of them does. The tables list the region where each item is found, along with the closest bell. All of them are visible on Steam.

Main Weapon achievements

Item descriptions detail the effects of several weapons. Jagged Steel’s slashes cause Bleeding, potentially resulting in Blood Loss. A drink activates the Fanatic’s Broken Blade, sending it hurtling toward faraway enemies; its attacks lose strength as the distance grows. Soft Snake Sword turns aside incoming enemy strikes. Outlaw Warblade can siphon foes’ blood to replenish HP, or use your HP to carve through enemies. Infernal Rouge is what Sanguine becomes through Tempering; its description reads: “A legendary weapon of the Bastion of Mó, concealing secrets tied to Soul’s origins. It can trigger Bloodline Rebirth and Bloodline Awakening.”

Phantom Edge achievements

For A Friend from Thin Air, any of these three Phantom Edges works. Each one’s description says it summons allies.

  • Kuye Temple Prayer Beads: Village of Lu; nearest bell: Smuggler’s Tunnel Entrance. Calls forth Kuye Temple’s deceased disciples.
  • Puppeteer’s Bell: Village of Lu; nearest bell: Broken Puppet Pit Bottom. Gives you command over Puppets of every kind.
  • Swordsman’s Summon: Mandate Encampment, closest bell Tower Rear Exit. Calls the Mandate’s disciples to fight.

How to unlock Defy Destiny

Defy Destiny is the game’s only Platinum-grade achievement, and it unlocks when you hold all 65 other achievements. Boss achievements and all story achievements except The Heart’s Journey stay hidden until earned.
